Questions

Good to know before you call

How fast can you get here?

For no-heat and no-cooling calls we aim to be there the same day, and we answer the phone 24 hours a day. Tune-ups and estimates are usually booked within the week.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

No. The price is the same at 2 PM on a Tuesday and 2 AM on a Sunday.

Are estimates free?

Estimates for a new furnace or air conditioner are free. For repairs there is a $79 diagnostic fee, and it comes off the bill if you go ahead with the repair.

Which brands do you work on?

All the major brands of furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, air conditioners and water heaters, whoever installed them.

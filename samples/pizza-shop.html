Baked in steel

Heavy steel pans, the way Detroit has baked its square pizza since the 1940s. They get hot and stay hot.

Cheese to the edge

Brick cheese goes right up to the wall of the pan, so every edge bakes into a crisp, caramelized crust.

Sauce on top

The red sauce goes on last, in two stripes. The crust stays crisp and the tomato stays bright.

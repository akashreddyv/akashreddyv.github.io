What you get

  • A website built around what you need, from a single page to a full business site.
  • Pages that load fast and work on every phone, tablet and computer.
  • Clear page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and structured data, so Google and Bing can read your site.
  • The features you need, like a contact form, a photo gallery or an admin page for updating your own text and photos.

Who it's for

Restaurants and cafes, trades and home services, shops, studios and salons, and anyone who needs a personal or portfolio site. If people look you up on their phone before they call, visit or buy, your website is often the first thing they see.

How it works

  1. You tell me about your business and what the site needs to do.
  2. I reply with questions and a quote.
  3. I build the site and send you a preview link, so you can try it on your own phone before anyone else sees it.
  4. When you're happy, it goes live on your domain, and I show you how to update it.

What I'll need from you

  • Your logo, if you have one.
  • Photos of your work, your place or your team. Phone photos are fine.
  • The text for each page, or notes I can turn into it.
  • Access to your domain name, if you already have one.

Questions

Do you build websites for small businesses?

Yes. I take on anything from a one-page site to a full business website, built around what you need.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. This website is my own work, and I built two sample sites for made-up businesses, a pizza shop and a heating and cooling company, to show what a small-business site can look like. They're on the projects page.

Will people be able to find my website on Google?

I can set up clear page titles and descriptions, a sitemap and structured data so Google and Bing can read your site, the same way this site is built. Rankings also depend on things outside the site, like links and reviews, but your site starts from a solid base.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if you'd like to. I can add an admin page where you change text and photos from your phone, like the one I use for this site.

Do I need a domain name?

Yes, a website needs an address such as yourbusiness.com. If you don't have one yet, I can help you choose one and set it up.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the text and photos are ready. A one-page site is much quicker than a full business website, and you'll get a timeline with the quote.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need, such as how many pages and which features. Tell me about your project through the contact page and I'll send you a quote.

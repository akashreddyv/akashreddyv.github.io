AI coaching

One-on-one or team coaching on using AI tools in your everyday work.

What we cover

  • Using AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude in your own work.
  • Writing better prompts.
  • Automating repeat tasks.
  • Knowing where AI helps, and where it doesn't.

Who it's for

People who have tried AI tools but don't use them day to day yet, small business owners who want to save time, and teams who want to agree on how they use AI at work.

Ideas for a session

  • Set up AI for your own job: your tasks, your tools and prompts you can reuse.
  • Take one repeat task and automate it from start to finish.
  • Write faster with AI: emails, proposals and reports that still sound like you.
  • For a team: where AI helps your team, where it doesn't, and simple ground rules.

One-on-one or as a team

Coaching is for one person or a whole team, and sessions can be in person in the Detroit area. I use AI to get from idea to working software fast, and the coaching is built on that experience. If you'd rather have something built for you, see custom AI agents.

Questions

Either: one-on-one sessions, or sessions for your team.

Which AI tools do you cover?

Mainly AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude, used in your own everyday work.

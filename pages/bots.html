Prediction-Market Bots

Two bots I built that watch short crypto prediction markets around the clock, test betting strategies against real prices, and place small real bets automatically once the data backs a strategy. The numbers here update about every 5 minutes.

Live · real money
Polymarket bot
5-minute "up or down" markets · BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP

Records every 5-minute window on Polymarket and Polymarket US, scores each strategy on real prices and fees, and trades the ones that hold up on Polymarket US with small real-money bets.

Kalshi bot
15-minute "price up?" markets · BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP

The same approach on Kalshi: it records every 15-minute window and checks its own numbers against Kalshi's official results before any strategy is trusted.

How the bots work

  1. Record everything. Every window for every coin: the market's prices several times a minute and the price feeds the market settles on.
  2. Test strategies on real prices. Each rule is scored as if it had bought at the real asking price, paid the exchange's real fees and held to the end.
  3. Beat a coin flip. A random-side control runs alongside. A strategy only counts if it beats it over weeks of data, not just a good weekend.
  4. Bet small, automatically. Only then does a bot trade real money: a couple of shares at a time, with hard limits on price, size and losses.

Research and education, not financial advice. Prediction markets are risky and past results don't predict future ones. Real bets are placed only on Polymarket US, a regulated US exchange. These pages show no personal or account information.

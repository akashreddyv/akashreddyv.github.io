Privacy & Cookies

What this site collects, what it stores in your browser, and how to reach me about either one.

What this site collects

The contact form sends your name, email address and message to me so I can reply. The suggestion box (and the terminal's suggest command) sends your suggestion, plus your name and email only if you give them. None of these is used for anything else, and I don't sell or share them.

The chat box (bottom right) answers common questions automatically, right in your browser; those questions aren't sent anywhere. Only when you choose Send to Akash does it email me your message, the chat so far and your email address if you add one. The conversation is kept in your browser's session storage so it survives moving between pages, and it's gone when you close the tab.

Everything else on this site — the pages, the projects, the referral codes — is just read, with no account and no sign-up.

Cookies & local storage

This site doesn't set tracking cookies. It keeps a few small values in your browser's own storage: your light/dark theme choice, whether you've already seen the header logo's animation once this visit, and the chat box conversation (until you close the tab). They stay on your device and clear themselves if you clear your browser's site data; the chat is only sent to me if you choose Send to Akash.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, and a few icons load from other CDNs (including simpleicons.org and unpkg.com, for the 3D logo viewer on the Projects page). Requesting a font or icon file tells that service your IP address, the same way loading any image from another site does. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with that — see Google's privacy policy for the fonts.

This site doesn't run analytics right now. If that changes, this page will say so, and I'd reach for something that doesn't need a cookie banner before anything that does.

Referral & affiliate links

The links and codes on /referrals go to other companies. Once you click through, that company's own privacy policy and cookies apply, not this one — check their site for details.

Your choices

Want to know what I have on file for you, or want it deleted? Ask through the contact page and I'll take care of it.

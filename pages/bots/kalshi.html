Kalshi Bot

Kalshi, a regulated US exchange, opens a "price up in the next 15 minutes?" market for Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and XRP four times an hour. This bot records every window, checks its own numbers against Kalshi's official results, and now tests the Polymarket bot's strategies on the recorded prices. A no-money dry run comes next.

Live picks under test

The side each picking rule would buy in the current 15-minute window on Kalshi, about a minute and a half before the close. The rules come from the Polymarket bot; on Kalshi their record is simulated on recorded prices over only a few days, so treat these picks as an experiment, not a tip. Prices move fast near the close: check the live price before buying anything.

Right now

The latest prices the bot saw in each coin's current window. "Price vs target" is how far the coin is from the price it has to finish at or above for "up" to win.

Official results vs the bot's own

When a window closes, the bot works out the result from its own price feeds and compares it with Kalshi's. The two must agree before any strategy is trusted; a miss usually means the price finished within a hair of the target.

Where it's at

Research and education, not financial advice. Prediction markets are risky and past results don't predict future ones. This bot places no bets yet. No personal or account information is shown.

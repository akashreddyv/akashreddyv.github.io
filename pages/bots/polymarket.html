What the testing shows so far

  1. Most ideas don't survive. Half of the strategies tested so far never beat the coin flip and were dropped. Only a few make it to real money.
  2. Win rate isn't the goal. A strategy can win nearly every bet and still lose money; the return on the money staked is what counts.
  3. A good weekend proves nothing. Results swing with the market's mood, so only weeks of data, real fees and a beaten control count.

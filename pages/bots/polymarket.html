Polymarket Bot

Every 5 minutes Polymarket opens a new "up or down?" market for Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and XRP. This bot records every one, scores each strategy on real prices and fees, and places small real-money bets on Polymarket US with the strategies that hold up.

Real bets

Every bet the bot has placed with real money since its first test, settled by the exchange. A couple of shares at a time: each share pays $1 if its side wins.

Strategy scoreboard

How each strategy would have done trading exactly like the bot: one bet per coin per window, 90 seconds before the end (60 if that price was missed), buying at the real asking price of at most 98¢, fees included, held to the end. "Profit per $1" is what each dollar bet made; the 95% range shows how sure that number is.

Cumulative profit of each strategy on Polymarket US at 2 shares a bet, since recording began.

What the data has taught me so far

  1. Losses are rare but big. Buying the favourite at 90¢+ wins most of the time, yet one loss can wipe out a dozen wins. Profit per dollar matters, not the count of losses.
  2. Weekends flatter. Quiet weekend markets made some strategies look great; busier weekday trading hours were much harder. Only weeks of data settle it.
  3. Stop-losses didn't help. Prices here jump rather than slide, so a stop usually sells after the drop, and it sells too many bets that would have won.
